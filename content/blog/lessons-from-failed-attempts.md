Failures are useful when they’re captured with enough context to improve decision-making.

## What I record

- Preconditions that were assumed (and were wrong).
- Environmental constraints (policy, EDR controls, identity posture).
- The *decision* that caused the failure.
- Defensive signals that showed up.

## What I avoid

- Step-by-step instructions that could be used outside authorized work.
- Sensitive identifiers, client naming, or unique environment fingerprints.

## Why this matters

A strong operator can explain **why** they chose a path, **how** they validated assumptions, and **what** would change the choice next time.
